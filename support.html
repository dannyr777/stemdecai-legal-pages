Support

Get help with your STEMDecAI account, learning content, subscription, app performance, or privacy request.

Support email: support@aivaryn.com.au

We aim to respond to support requests within 2 to 3 business days.

Before contacting support

  1. Update STEMDecAI through the Apple App Store or Google Play.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Confirm that your internet connection is working.
  4. Sign out and sign in again, where safe to do so.
  5. Check whether the problem occurs on another network.

What to include

  • Your registered email address, if you have an account.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • Your STEMDecAI app version.
  • A clear description of what happened and what you expected.
  • The steps needed to reproduce the issue.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, provided it does not expose sensitive information.

Never send your password, payment card number, authentication code, or sensitive personal information by email.

Common support areas

Account access

Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. Contact support if you no longer have access to your registered email address.

Learning content or AI-generated answers

Report inaccurate, unsafe, or unsuitable content and include the subject, deck, question, and screenshot. AI-generated content should be checked against reliable educational sources.

Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are normally billed through Apple or Google and may be managed through RevenueCat or another subscription provider where used. You can manage or cancel a subscription through your device's App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel a subscription.

Refund eligibility and processing are generally controlled by the store through which the purchase was made. Include the transaction reference when contacting support, but do not send full payment details.

Notifications

Check both the app's notification settings and your device notification permissions.

Data and Account Deletion

STEMDecAI is designed to be privacy-first. The app does not require or support user account registration (there is no sign-in or profile database). Because of this, we do not store personal account credentials on our servers.

How to delete your data:

  • Local Data (History, Progress, Settings): All of your study history, quiz progress, and configuration settings are stored locally on your device. You can permanently delete this data at any time by going to the app's settings and selecting Clear History, or by simply uninstalling the application from your device.
  • Subscription and Telemetry Data: Any anonymous data associated with device diagnostics, app analytics, or subscription status (handled securely via Google Play, the App Store, or our payment processors) can be requested for deletion by emailing us at support@aivaryn.com.au.

We will process and confirm all data deletion requests within 30 days.

Please include:

  • The email address associated with the account.
  • A clear statement that you want the account deleted.
  • Enough information for us to verify that you control the account.

We may contact you to verify the request. Eligible account data will be deleted or de-identified, subject to information we must retain for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial recordkeeping purposes.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel the subscription separately in the relevant store.
