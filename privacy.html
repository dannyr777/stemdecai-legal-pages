Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aivaryn Pty Ltd collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use STEMDecAI.

STEMDecAI is an educational support tool. It does not replace a teacher, school curriculum, professional tutor, or independent verification of academic information.

1. Who we are

STEMDecAI is operated by Aivaryn Pty Ltd, based in Canberra, Australian Capital Territory, Australia. In this policy, "STEMDecAI", "we", "us", and "our" refer to Aivaryn Pty Ltd and the STEMDecAI service.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the STEMDecAI mobile application, related websites, customer support, and services that link to this policy.

3. Information we collect or may collect

Account and profile information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address and authentication information.
  • Age range, school year, or learning level, only where the app asks for it.
  • Account preferences and communication settings.

Learning and app activity

  • Subjects, topics, decks, flashcards, quizzes, and revision activities used, where those features are enabled.
  • Answers, scores, progress, streaks, bookmarks, and learning preferences, where the app saves them.
  • Prompts, questions, or content you submit to AI-supported features.
  • Content you create, save, upload, or generate through the app.

Subscription and transaction information

Purchases may be processed by Apple, Google, and, if used in the app, RevenueCat or another authorised subscription management provider. We may receive transaction status, subscription level, renewal status, product identifier, and a transaction reference. We do not normally receive your full payment card number.

Device, diagnostic, and usage information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, and region.
  • IP address, general location derived from IP, and security logs.
  • Crash reports, performance information, and feature interaction data, if analytics or crash reporting tools such as Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics are enabled.
  • Advertising or analytics identifiers, only where advertising or analytics are enabled and permitted.

Support information

When you contact us, we may collect your email address, message, screenshots, device details, and other information needed to investigate the request.

4. How we collect information

We may collect information:

  • Directly from you when you register, use a feature, or contact support.
  • Automatically through the app and associated technologies.
  • From Apple, Google, authentication providers, subscription providers such as RevenueCat where used, analytics providers, and service providers.

5. Why we use information

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your account.
  • To deliver flashcards, quizzes, revision tools, progress tracking, and AI-supported learning features.
  • To personalise subjects, difficulty, recommendations, and revision activities, where those features are enabled.
  • To process and manage subscriptions and purchases.
  • To provide customer support and service communications.
  • To improve reliability, security, accessibility, and product performance.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

6. AI-supported features

STEMDecAI may use artificial intelligence to generate flashcards, quizzes, explanations, revision breakdowns, examples, and memory prompts. Information submitted to these features may be processed by technology providers acting on our behalf.

AI requests may include the prompt or question you submit, selected learning context, generated content, and technical data needed to operate and secure the request. We do not intentionally send payment card details or account passwords to AI providers.

AI-generated content can be incomplete or incorrect. Users should check important educational information against reliable sources, curriculum materials, or a qualified educator.

Do not enter highly sensitive personal information, passwords, financial details, health information, or confidential school information into an AI-supported feature.

7. When we may share information

We may share information with:

  • Cloud hosting, database, authentication, communication, security, analytics, and technical support providers.
  • AI service providers used to deliver requested AI-supported features.
  • Apple, Google, RevenueCat where used, and subscription or purchase management providers.
  • Advertising providers, only where advertising is used, disclosed, and permitted.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, courts, law enforcement, or government authorities where required or permitted by law.
  • A purchaser or successor if the business is reorganised, merged, sold, or transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information for money.

8. Advertising, analytics, and crash tools

STEMDecAI may use analytics and crash tools, such as Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics if enabled, to understand app performance, diagnose faults, and improve reliability. If the app displays advertising, ads may be contextual or personalised depending on the released app configuration, your device settings, region, age, and consent choices.

If analytics, crash reporting, or advertising are not enabled in the production app, this policy should be updated before publication to remove those references.

9. Children and younger users

STEMDecAI may contain learning content suitable for students, but account eligibility and parental consent requirements depend on the released app configuration and applicable law. A child should use the service only with the involvement and permission of a parent, guardian, school, or other authorised adult where required.

Users must not misrepresent their age. We may restrict features, disable advertising personalisation, request consent, or close an account where required to protect younger users or comply with law.

10. International processing

Some service providers may process or store information outside Australia. Privacy protections and legal access rules may differ between countries. We take reasonable steps to use appropriate providers and safeguards.

11. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards intended to protect information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal and accounting obligations, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by information type.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it or de-identify it, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Access or correct account information.
  • Change notification, advertising, analytics, or device permissions.
  • Request access to or correction of personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where a legal right applies.
  • Make a privacy complaint.

14. Account and data deletion

You can request account deletion through Profile > Settings > Legal and Support > Delete Account in the app, where available, or through our account deletion page.

We may need to verify your identity. Some information may be retained where required for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or financial recordkeeping. De-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked back to you may be retained.

15. Complaints

Contact us first so we can investigate and respond. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or another relevant regulator.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be published here with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email.
